
# Camera Couldn't Open – Android Troubleshooting

**Date:** July 28 (year to verify)  
**Category:** Mobile / Android  
**Status:** Solved ✅

## 1. Problem

The phone's camera application failed to open.

Error message:

> Camera couldn't open. Check your phone.

## 2. Investigation

I started investigating the camera-related settings and permissions.

Things I checked:
- WhatsApp camera functionality.
- WhatsApp camera access permissions.
- Camera application settings.

## 3. Findings

I noticed that camera access permission was denied ("Don't allow") in the permission settings.

## 4. Troubleshooting

Actions recorded in my notebook:

1. Checked WhatsApp camera permissions.
2. Investigated the Camera application settings.
3. Cleared the Camera application's data.

## 5. Result

**Problem Solved!** 🎉

The camera started working again.

## 6. Lessons Learned

- Application permissions can affect access to hardware features.
- Multiple applications may depend on the same hardware resource.
- Checking permissions and application configuration is a useful first troubleshooting step.
